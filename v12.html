Before real learners join

  • Publish privacy notice, controller contact, purposes, lawful basis, retention schedule and subprocessors.
  • Support access/export, correction and verified deletion requests.
  • Keep evidence files in private storage and issue short-lived signed links after permission checks.
  • Never place credentials, learner records or evidence in public repositories or analytics.
  • Complete accessibility, security, backup/restore and cross-tenant access testing.
