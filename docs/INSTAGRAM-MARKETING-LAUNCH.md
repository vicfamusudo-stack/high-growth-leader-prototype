# High Growth Leader™ — Instagram Launch and Lead Capture Plan

**Status:** Content and landing-page draft created; lead capture is not live until an endpoint, privacy notice, contact address and tests are completed.

## Campaign brief

- **Audience:** Business owners and founders.
- **Content style:** Faceless Reels, text animations, practical carousels, voiceover and office/workflow B-roll.
- **Primary conversion:** Early-access waiting-list registration.
- **Core message:** Build leaders who build leaders. Build a business that grows beyond you.
- **Brand direction:** Light warm-neutral surfaces for educational carousels; deep navy, ivory and restrained teal for brand moments; readable high-contrast typography.
- **Marketing approach:** Organic validation first. Do not spend on paid ads until message, landing page and lead capture are validated.

## Lead capture launch checklist

- [ ] Choose the form/CRM provider and create a secure HTTPS endpoint.
- [ ] Configure the endpoint to validate fields server-side, protect against spam, and store only necessary data.
- [ ] Configure a notification or secure dashboard so submissions are actually seen.
- [ ] Add the endpoint to `window.HGL_LEADS_ENDPOINT` in `early-access.html`, or replace the front-end seam with the provider's approved embed/API.
- [ ] Publish a real privacy notice and contact email; replace placeholder footer contact link.
- [ ] Keep optional marketing consent separate from the request to join early access; record the consent text, timestamp and source where appropriate.
- [ ] Define retention, access, deletion and unsubscribe handling before collecting real personal data.
- [ ] Test valid submission, invalid email, missing required fields, spam trap, endpoint failure, success response, mobile layout and keyboard/screen-reader use.
- [ ] Confirm confirmation email or thank-you flow works.
- [ ] Only then publish the link in Instagram bio and in campaign posts.

**Recommended minimum form fields:** first name, email, biggest leadership challenge. Business name and team size are optional. Avoid collecting confidential employee information.

### Reel 2 — The promotion trap (20 sec)
> Being your best employee doesn't automatically make you your best manager.

> Before the next promotion, ask: can they coach, delegate, make sound decisions and take responsibility for outcomes?  
> Don't just promote potential. Develop leadership capability.  
> Follow High Growth Leader for more.

### Reel 3 — Early access (20–25 sec; use only once capture works)

> We're developing a new approach to building leaders who build leaders.  
> We're inviting business owners to follow the journey and register their interest in early access.  
> Because better leadership should show up in the real world.  
> Join the early-access list through our bio.

## Production specification

- Reels: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 export, captions/subtitles, hook in first 1–2 seconds, clear voiceover, low music level, branded end card.
- Use original footage or appropriately licensed stock video and audio.
- Use high-contrast type, short lines and generous safe margins for Instagram interface overlays.
- Carousels: 1080×1350 portrait; one idea per slide, high contrast, last slide has a relevant save/share/follow CTA.
- Use clear alt text where available; caption videos; avoid flashing or overly rapid transitions.
- Label product concepts honestly; don't imply unfinished features are already available.

## Measurement

Review weekly:
- reach and non-follower reach
- average watch time and completion
- saves and shares
- profile visits and follows
- bio-link clicks
- landing-page sessions
- completed registrations
- qualified founder/pilot conversations

Use UTM-tagged bio links and, where available, privacy-conscious analytics. Optimise for qualified registrations and conversations, not follower count alone. Do not set arbitrary lead targets until a baseline exists.

## Launch gates

**Gate A — ready for content production:** brand/profile copy, content calendar, Reel scripts and landing-page draft are ready.

**Gate B — ready to invite sign-ups:** lead endpoint connected, privacy notice/contact details published, submissions tested, notification/review process working.

**Gate C — ready to scale:** baseline data collected, audience-message fit reviewed, qualified leads engaged, and paid spend considered only if organic validation supports it.
